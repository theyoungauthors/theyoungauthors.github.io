Where Young Voices Become Lasting Legacies

About the Project

A nation's stories are shaped by its people. By encouraging young voices to write, reflect, and publish, Qatar invests in a generation capable of expressing its identity while engaging confidently with the wider world.

Inspiring Authors. Preserving Heritage. Shaping the Future.

Mission

To advance human development by empowering youth through authorship, fostering critical thinking, creativity and cultural stewardship, and enabling every voice to contribute meaningfully to national identity and the shared story of humanity.

Vision

A world in which every generation is empowered through authorship is one that preserves its heritage, enriches its communities, and leaves a lasting legacy of knowledge, creativity, and understanding for generations to come.

Our Pedagogy

The Young Authors Project Framework™

The Young Authors Project Framework™ uses a student-centred, project-based learning framework that guides learners from idea to publication of an original work. Through six progressive stages, students develop creative thinking, planning, literacy, communication, critical thinking and project-management skills.

Program Delivery

School Delivery Model

Delivered through a structured school-based model that supports consistent implementation across learning environments:

  • Weekly guided writing sessions
  • Facilitator-led workshops
  • Teacher implementation guides
  • School support and learning materials
  • Simultaneous delivery in English and Arabic
